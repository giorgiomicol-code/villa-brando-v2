Privacy policy

How we handle the data of website visitors and of people who contact us.

Last updated: September 2026

This notice is provided under Articles 13 and 14 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) to visitors of www.villabrando.com and to anyone who contacts Villa Brando through the website.

1. Data controller

The data controller is the operator of Villa Brando Exclusive, Via Torino 19, 04100 Latina (LT), Italy. CIN IT059011C27L4KEECL. For any privacy request: villabrando.italy@gmail.com, phone +39 351 976 8732.

2. What data we process

  • Browsing data. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.). As with any website, the server logs technical data such as IP address, date and time of the request and browser type, to keep the service running and secure.
  • Data you send us. If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we process the data you provide (name, contact details, stay dates, number of guests, message content) in order to reply.
  • Booking data. The "Find your perfect date" form does not store anything on our website: the dates and number of guests you enter are passed to the official Direct-Book booking engine (direct-book.com), where the booking is completed. Bookings via Airbnb or Booking.com take place on their websites.

3. Why we process it and on what legal basis

  • Answering your requests and giving you information about your stay: pre-contractual steps taken at your request (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).
  • Managing the booking and the stay: performance of the contract (Art. 6.1.b) and legal obligations, such as reporting guests to the public security authority, tax obligations and the tourist tax (Art. 6.1.c).
  • Keeping the website running and secure: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f).

4. Cookies and third-party content

The website does not use profiling cookies or any analytics or advertising tools. Some pages load third-party content:

  • Google Fonts (Google), for typefaces: your browser connects to Google’s servers, which receive your IP address.
  • YouTube videos, embedded in privacy-enhanced mode (youtube-nocookie.com): YouTube may store information on your device only when you start playback.
  • The Tours & Experiences page stores only your chosen language in the browser (localStorage), so it is shown again on your next visit.

Links to external websites (Direct-Book, Airbnb, Booking.com, GetYourGuide, WhatsApp, Instagram, Google Maps, Google Drive) lead to services with their own privacy policies, which we invite you to read.

5. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Data may be processed by the providers of the services listed above (hosting, email, messaging, booking platforms), by our accountant for tax obligations and by the competent authorities where required by law. Some providers, such as GitHub and Google, may process data in the United States, under the safeguards provided by the GDPR (for example the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses).

6. How long we keep data

Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to reply and to manage any resulting stay. Booking-related data are kept for the period required by law, in particular tax law (normally 10 years). Technical browsing data are handled by the hosting provider according to its own policies.

7. Your rights

You may at any time request access to your data, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interest, by writing to villabrando.italy@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (www.garanteprivacy.it).

8. Changes

This notice may be updated. The version in force is always the one published on this page.
