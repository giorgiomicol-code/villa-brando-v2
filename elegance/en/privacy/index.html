Privacy policy

How we handle the data of website visitors and of guests staying at Villa Brando.

Privacy e cookie policy

This information is provided pursuant to Reg. (EU) 2016/679 of 27/04/2016 on the protection of individuals with regard to the processing of personal data (GDPR), and contains information on the processing of personal data collected by the Villa Brando Holiday Home.

In any case, all the data acquired will be processed in compliance with the GDPR, as well as according to the canons of confidentiality inherent in the performance of the activity.

Personal data may be made available to the Judicial Authority and/or the Police Forces, upon their specific request, to comply with legal or regulatory requirements or for the purpose of identifying the authors of any unlawful acts committed to the detriment of the Holiday Home.

The treatment will preferably be carried out electronically. In particular, a form may be sent by SMS or WhatsApp for the guest to fill in themselves, or the same data may be collected by dedicated personnel on arrival, including on a paper form.

Nature of personal data, purposes and recipients of processing

The personal data collected is processed only for the communication to the Police Authorities (AlloggiatiWEB portal) about your presence on site, or for sending access credentials to the electronic locks of the holiday home. The same data will not be retained or recorded for other purposes. Personal data will not be disseminated, unless required by law or expressly authorised.

Data retention

Considering the purposes for which they were collected, the fulfilment of legal obligations or the protection of the rights of the owner, such data will be kept for a period not exceeding that necessary, and in any case in line with the term allowed by current law.

With specific reference to video surveillance, the outdoor areas of the property are monitored by security cameras; footage collected is kept for no more than 24 hours and will not be disseminated or communicated to third parties, except for police or justice needs.

Data Controller

For any further information you can contact the Data Controller, Casa Vacanze Villa Brando, by writing to villabrando.italy@gmail.com. You can contact this address to assert your rights, in particular to access your personal data, and to request rectification, cancellation or portability, limitation of processing, or to oppose it. Please include your name, email address, postal address and telephone number, so that your request can be handled correctly. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the competent data protection authority.

Acceptance

By visiting the Villa Brando website you confirm that you have read and understood this statement.
