Sea and nature

Coast, parks and islands

Lido di Latina, its coastal lakes and dunes form a unique, protected environment. Sabaudia, San Felice Circeo and Terracina are among the nearest seaside destinations. You can explore the Garden of Ninfa, the Pontine Islands and Circeo National Park, with its dunes, lakes and woods.

Sermoneta, Cori, Norma, Terracina, Gaeta and Fondi complete an area rich in historic places and varied landscapes.
