Ideal for families, groups of friends, business stays, corporate teams, smart working, film and TV locations, photo shoots and events (on request).

Discover the unique charm of Villa Brando, an outstanding holiday residence. Enjoy the comfort of spacious interiors, carefully designed rooms and a lush garden, just minutes from Sabaudia, Rome and the beaches of the Lazio coast and the Pontine Plain.

An authentic example of 1970s Italian architecture, the villa has been renovated while keeping its original character intact: a precise, deliberate choice, appreciated by those who look for personality rather than standardisation. It also features home automation, including electronic locks, for a comfortable, carefree stay. It is set over three levels and surrounded by its own private garden.

The Italian coast, nature and history

Beaches, villages, lakes, parks and authentic experiences just minutes away.

Just a few kilometres away you can immerse yourself in the incredible beauty of historic towns such as Sermoneta, Cori, Norma, Terracina, Gaeta and Fondi. You can explore natural sites such as the Oasis of Ninfa, the Pontine Islands and the vast Circeo National Park, which stretches along the coast between Latina and Sabaudia with dunes, lakes and woods.
