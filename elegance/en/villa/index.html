Villa Brando

An exclusive, sustainable holiday home

A finely furnished holiday home in Latina, near Sabaudia, with three large, bright living rooms overlooking the garden that will win you over with their architecture and furnishings.

With four spacious bedrooms, a walk-in wardrobe and a games room, this holiday home offers a relaxing and enjoyable stay for up to 9 guests.

Villa Brando is just a 20-minute drive from the beautiful beaches of Sabaudia, Terracina and San Felice Circeo, in the city of Latina on the magnificent Pontine coast.

Villa Brando is the ideal choice for a quality holiday built around independence and tranquillity. Being close to the city centre gives easy access to sports centres, riding stables and entertainment venues, both in town and along the coast.
