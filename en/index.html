An authentic Italian villa

Personality, space and quiet.

Villa Brando is an authentic example of 1970s Italian architecture, renovated while preserving its original character: a deliberate choice for those who value personality over standardisation.

The villa spans three levels and is surrounded by its own secluded garden. Its generous interiors combine carefully chosen furnishings with smart home technology and electronic locks.
